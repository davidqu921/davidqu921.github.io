Hello, I’m David

I’m a fourth-year Computer Science student at the University of Toronto Scarborough. My work sits at the intersection of applied AI, reliable generative systems, and healthcare.

In Summer 2026, I am a CRA Undergraduate Research Experience (URE) researcher, focusing on the evaluation and risk management of generative AI. I also work as a Teaching Assistant at UTSC. Previously, I spent eight months in Shanghai building and deploying medical imaging models as an AI Algorithm Engineer.

Experience

CRA Undergraduate Research Experience Researcher

Summer 2026 · University of Toronto Scarborough

  • Researching methods for evaluating generative AI systems and managing their risks.
  • Supported by the Computing Research Association’s Undergraduate Research Experience program.

Teaching Assistant

University of Toronto Scarborough

  • Support undergraduate Computer Science teaching and student learning.
  • Have led course review sessions for more than 100 students.

AI Algorithm Engineer

January–August 2025 · Yangtze River Delta Guozhi Intelligent Medical Technology · Shanghai, China

  • Built medical imaging models for CT organ segmentation, AMD detection, and H&E histology classification.
  • Helped deploy AI solutions at Huashan Hospital, affiliated with Fudan University.
  • Improved classification Dice scores by 3% with a multi-branch neural network.
  • Designed a ViT–CNN hybrid architecture with multi-head self-attention.

SQL Database Developer

January–August 2023 · York Region District School Board

  • Completed more than five projects involving data extraction, processing, and systems integration.
  • Worked with Microsoft SQL Server and educational data systems.

Selected Research and Projects

Generative AI Evaluation and Risk Management

My current research focuses on making generative AI systems more reliable and suitable for real-world deployment.

Medical AI Systems

I have worked on medical image analysis, a rare-disease literature system built with RAG and PubMed data, and a multi-agent rehabilitation workflow using CrewAI, LangChain, and MCP.

Nuclear Segmentation and Classification

I contributed to Exploring Architectural Enhancements for HoVer-Net: Deep Learning-based Method of Automated Nuclear Segmentation and Classification.
